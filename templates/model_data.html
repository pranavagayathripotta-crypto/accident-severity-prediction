06

Decision threshold

The threshold controls when the model changes the output from minor risk to elevated risk.

{{ (stats.threshold * 100)|round|int }}% ELEVATED-RISK TRIGGER
The model flags elevated risk once its predicted probability passes {{ (stats.threshold * 100)|round|int }}%, rather than waiting for the default 50% threshold.

With this setting, the threshold evaluation reports {{ stats.threshold_recall }}% recall for real elevated-risk crashes, with {{ stats.threshold_precision }}% precision .

This is intentionally oriented toward early caution rather than a definitive accident decision.
